§13.3 Architectural Rationale

Why a light wallet cannot tell “nobody paid you” from “the server left your payment out”.

In BIP-352 silent payments, receivers do not provide addresses up front. Senders compute ephemeral outputs derived from scan keys. To detect funds, light wallets query untrusted indexers for public tweak data.

If an indexer omits a tweak record, the client simply reconstructs fewer transactions. The failure mode is totally silent: balances appear smaller with zero protocol violation signaled.

format_quote Bitshala BIP-352 Architecture Guide
“Without committed receipts, a malicious or degraded indexer can perform a surgical eclipse: simply returning empty tweak lists. The client cannot distinguish between zero transaction volume and an indexer actively omitting outputs.”

§13.4 Verification Topology

The 4-Step Loop & Canonical States

01 · COMMIT

Indexer signs the Merkle root of all valid tweaks for each regtest block tip.

02 · DECLARE

Client requests tweak ranges, receiving cryptographically bound response receipts.

03 · RECOMPUTE

Client recomputes the expected tweak tree locally from independent headers.

04 · PROVE

Inconsistencies produce a compact portable fraud proof exportable to other relays.

The 6 Discrete Verification States
Checked

Every served tweak matches indexer signature and header commitments. Balance is accountable.

Checked Gap Filled

Temporary omission repaired via secondary peer relay before window expiration.

Can’t Be Checked

Required headers or parent commitments pruned past the active 144-block verification window.

Not Checked

Proof payload downloaded but verification queue not yet executed locally.

Servers Disagree

Two indexed relays signed conflicting Merkle roots for the identical block height.

Data Withheld

Indexer refused to serve entries beneath a published root hash. Balance marked lower bound.

§13.6 Transparent Boundary Architecture

Limits Next to Claims

Cryptographic tools must document explicit failure limits directly alongside their guarantees.

Area of Claim What Canary Proves Invariable Protocol Boundary
Output-side withholding Evaluates completeness of tweak generation for registered scan keys. Deferred to v2 spec. Current system checks tweak lists, not UTXO spending status.
Cut-through hash withholding Pinpoints exact missing entries if the full tweak list is fetched. If only truncated 32-byte hashes are requested, withholding is flagged but unresolvable.
Single-server eclipse Detects non-responsiveness and marks local ledger as lower-bound. Requires at least 1 honest indexer or an uncensored peer path to restore state.
